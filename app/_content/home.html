Methodology — following structured patterns to maximize the coverage surface.

Engagements follow the PTES phase model. Every step generates artifacts: pcaps, screenshots, command logs, CVE references.

00

Scoping & rules of engagement

Define targets, exclusions, windows, escalation contacts. Authorization in writing before a single packet leaves the lab.

03

Exploitation

Leveraging known CVEs, web vulnerabilities (SQLi / SSRF / LFI / deserialization), credential stuffing, AS-REP roasting, Kerberoasting.

01

Reconnaissance

Passive OSINT — domains, subdomains, certificates, leaked credentials, employee footprint — then active enumeration.

04

Post-exploitation & pivoting

Credential harvesting, lateral movement, AD attacks (DCSync, unconstrained delegation), tunneled access into segmented networks.

02

Scanning & enumeration

Services fingerprinting, versions detection, fuzzing, bruteforcing, enumeration, anonymous sessions.

05

Reporting

A clear, concise, actionable document aimed at both technical and non-technical figures consolidating all findings.

/ 03

AI — The future is now.

AI isn't just a productivity tool — it's an offensive multiplier. These aren't hypotheticals.

⚡
Social Engineering

Hyper-personalised spear phishing

LLMs trained on OSINT data generate bespoke lures — correct tone, internal jargon, plausible pretexts — at industrial scale. A single operator can run thousands of simultaneous, contextually aware campaigns that previously required a full team.

Risk: credential harvest · BEC fraud · initial access
🎭
Identity Deception

Real-time deepfake impersonation

Live voice and video synthesis can impersonate a CFO on a call or clone a colleague's face in a video conference. Attackers have already used AI-generated audio to authorise fraudulent wire transfers in the tens of millions.

Risk: wire fraud · vishing · insider trust abuse
🔍
Vulnerability Research

Automated zero-day discovery

AI-assisted fuzzing and code analysis compress months of manual vulnerability research into hours. State-sponsored groups are already using LLM pipelines to triage large codebases and surface exploitable memory-safety issues before patches exist.

Risk: 0-day weaponisation · supply chain · critical infra
🦠
Malware Development

Polymorphic, self-rewriting malware

AI can rewrite malware signatures on every execution cycle, staying ahead of static detection. Coupled with autonomous C2 logic, it reduces the need for live operator interaction — making attribution and disruption significantly harder.

Risk: AV/EDR evasion · persistent access · ransomware
⚔️
Cyberwarfare

Autonomous offensive agents

Nation-state actors are deploying AI agents capable of conducting full attack chains — recon, exploitation, lateral movement, exfiltration — with minimal human oversight. The attack loop shrinks from days to minutes. Defence cannot yet match this speed.

Risk: critical infrastructure · defence systems · sovereignty
📡
Disinformation

AI-generated influence operations

Synthetic personas, AI-written propaganda, and manufactured media are deployed at scale to destabilise elections, erode trust in institutions, and manipulate public perception. Cyberwarfare is no longer just about systems — it targets human cognition.

Risk: societal trust · democratic processes · national security
